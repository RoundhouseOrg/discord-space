# Market research

Reviews read on top.gg on 2026-08-29 (10 for Epic RPG, 6 for Pokétwo, 8 for
IdleRPG), plus CBR's editorial review of Epic RPG and listings on
alternative.me / discordbotlist.

## The three bots

| Bot | Servers | Rating | Reviews | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pokétwo | ~1.5M | 4.54 | 3,872 | Active, huge |
| Epic RPG | ~608K | 4.54 | 2,447 | Active |
| IdleRPG | ~235K | 4.53 | 579 | Effectively dead ("RIP… no longer functional", "the bot left my servers") |

Other names in the genre: Dank Memer (economy), PokéMeow, Conquest, IDLE FARM,
TacoShack, BattleBanana, UltimateRPG.

## What reviewers actually said

### Epic RPG
- Positive: "Once you learn all the commands it's addictive." "Es muy adictivo…
  la progresión es muy limpia y divertida." "Best experience." "It's fun."
- Negative — every single one is about vote rewards or cooldowns: "Didn't get
  the rewards for voting", "cooldown not reset", "Cooldowns didn't reset",
  "hope gt better reward". One: "Does this bot not have a dashboard??"
- CBR: addictive, absurdist humor (Mutant Water Bottles, Raging Burritos), but
  "no ongoing narrative", and multiple players flood a channel.
- Ecosystem note: third-party companion bots exist (Epic RPG Guide, Epic RPG
  stats, Epic RPG Remind) — a sign of both depth and of missing built-in UX.

### Pokétwo
- Most-upvoted review (62 upvotes): "Said I voted but I didn't get a reward."
- "playing for free doesn't give much, players who pay have unmatchable
  advantage… hidden behind paywall feel" — from a 2021-era player who also
  praises the art team.
- "Toxic community and terrible battle mechanics."
- "toxic gatekeeping user within an hour… got away with it after I reported him."
- "I got banned even though I didn't cheat."
- alternative.me: "extreme lags… 69517ms."

### IdleRPG
- Praise: "does not require too much of your time. Still there are things to
  do. It is addictive. The end-game is a little grindy… you never get behind."
  "Endless commands."
- Criticism: "$help response is too long and difficult for beginners",
  "requires too many people to play", "never figure out how to make command",
  "It just isn't creating my character" (two separate people), repeated
  vote-crate glitches.

## Synthesis

### What works
1. **Cooldown-gated actions** — the source of "addictive" in nearly every
   positive review. Habit loops without long sessions.
2. **A short, learnable command vocabulary.** The fun starts *after*
   onboarding.
3. **Personality.** Absurd mobs, cute art — flavor is the only *content*
   reviewers praise. Numbers alone don't get praised.
4. **Cross-server persistent identity.** Your character follows your account;
   that's how these spread.

### What doesn't work (by frequency)
1. **Vote-reward / cooldown bugs** — #1 complaint on all three bots.
2. **Pay-to-win perception** (Pokétwo).
3. **Community moderation failures** — trading/PvP brings toxicity, scams,
   false-positive bans; players blame the bot.
4. **Onboarding failure** (IdleRPG) — prefix commands + long help.
5. **Requires a crowd** (IdleRPG) — features needing many players are dead in
   small servers.
6. **Channel spam / no narrative** (Epic RPG).
7. **Reliability at scale** — Pokétwo lag; IdleRPG's death.

### What's unique per bot
- Epic RPG: humor + dense crafting/gathering economy.
- Pokétwo: **ambient spawns triggered by chat activity**; art; trading;
  global marketplace.
- IdleRPG: true idle design (adventures resolve on a timer) + social systems
  (marriage, guilds, classes/races).

### Barriers to entry
- For players: memorizing commands (high for prefix, low for slash), needing
  enough people, cooldown/vote friction. The first 5 minutes decide it.
- For a new bot: incumbents are 5–7 years old with wikis and companion bots.
  Competing on breadth is hopeless. The open lane is: slash-command onboarding
  that works, real tactical combat with buttons, a deep stats/social layer,
  and no reward bugs. None of the three offer tactical combat.
